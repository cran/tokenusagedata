# tokenusagedata 0.1.0

* Initial release with five data frames and 154 records from the October
  2026 snapshots of tokenusage.dev research notes 002, 003 and 004.
* Includes 88 token-price records, four premium-price records, 25 context
  windows, 28 published model sizes and nine model-size estimates.
* Preserves source text and URLs, converts empty fields to missing values,
  and adds documented dates, nominal numeric quantities and lab fields.
* Uses the three Zenodo version records and SHA-256-pinned source CSV files.
